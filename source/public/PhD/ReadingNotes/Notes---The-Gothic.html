    This chapter introduces the rest of the book which is divided into tropes or themes connected to the gothic as a genre. The term however is slippery, and enbodies an atmosphere more than it does cover a particular movement or technique. There is overlap with the uncanny, abject and horror in general, also sci fi. This imprecision is not a bad thing, but potentially accounts for its recurrence in art and pop culture in times of political, artistic and technological crises. There’s a flamboyance, adolenscence in some ways, but it can be subtle and sophisticated. Art is often not explicitly called gothic (even if it is), so its an imperfect and hard to quantify term, although it does have roots in anglosaxon literary tradition (although there are mexican and south american Catholicism based works also).
