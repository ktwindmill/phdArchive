This text is an excerpt, an appraisal of how collage is a challenge to painting. I'm not sure if the author is reluctant or disapproving of the artist becoming divorced from their canvas, or whether that taking and improving (developing), is the morality of collage. Is the difference between appropriation of a ready made/an object etc the idea that it was not and is being transformed into an art work. Is it the sheer scale of appropriation and homogeneity that it caused the issue? 


**Raoul Hausmann Photomontage 1931**

> ([[Appropriation.pdf#page=16&selection=238,16,240,13&color=yellow|Appropriation, p.16]])
> But just as revolutionary as the content of photomontage was its form - photography and printed texts combined and transformed into a kind of static film. 

This text is about the potential of photomontage, a response to the technique being passe perhaps, or old hat. The author argues that as a reflection of environment, as our political, social and psycological structures change, so will photomontage be available to develop, offering alternative (literal) perspectives, contrasts in form and texture.
