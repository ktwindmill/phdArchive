## Search tips / strings for database searches

- **Identify Keywords:** Break your topic into main concepts and list synonyms, alternative spellings, and acronyms for each.
- **Boolean Operators (AND, OR, NOT):**
    - **AND** narrows results (e.g., _diet_ **AND** _obesity_).
    - **OR** broadens results with synonyms (e.g., _teenager_ **OR** _adolescent_).
    - **NOT** excludes irrelevant terms.
- **Phrase Searching (" "):** Use quotation marks to find exact phrases, such as `"global warming"` or `"climate change"`.
- **Truncation/Wildcards (*, ?):** Use a symbol (often `*`) to find word variations (e.g., `educat*` finds education, educate, educational). Use `?` for single character variations (e.g., `m?n` for man or men).
- **Nesting (Parentheses):** Group synonyms together with OR inside parentheses to combine them with other concepts (e.g., `(cancer OR tumor) AND therapy`)
