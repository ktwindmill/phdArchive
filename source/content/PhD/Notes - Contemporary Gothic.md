> > To revive is to assume fresh life (or indeed, to give fresh life). As frequent readers of Gothic fiction will know, such returns from the dead are staple features of Gothic narrative; but like Frankenstein’s monster, these revivals seldom take exactly the same shape they possessed before. The notion of revival can be seen to imply a reappropriation and reinvention of previous forms rather than a straightforward repetition.
