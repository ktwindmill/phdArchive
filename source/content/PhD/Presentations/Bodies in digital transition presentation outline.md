THE MEAT 

- Propose reading this condition through the gothic: a genre historically preoccupied with unstable authorship, from Walpole's fraudulent framing of The Castle of Otranto as a found manuscript onward, and with architectures of hidden, unindexed space, doubles, dopplegangers and secret histories. (see also the Woman in White, The King in Yellow, Frankenstein, Dr Jekyll & Mr Hyde etc) 
    

- Short overview of why I'm interested in the gothic and how it is defined:  
    

- Cross disciplinary: Film, literature, fashion, music 
    

- Slippery in definition, and difficult to apply to visual arts 
    

- Horror community and fan culture, space for the queer uncanny 
    

- The economics of horror 
    

- Specific gothic tropes of generational secrets, misauthored/misinterpreted text, obsession, recurrance (history repeating itself), doubles and dopplegangers 
    

- Artifice and theatricality, deliberate gaslighting/misleading of the protagonist 
    

- Can these tropes be pivoted to describe something happening in generative system assisted works? 
    

- Linking to genAI and authorship  
    

- Style and trope surfacing in GenAI output with no traceable citation or intent behind them 
    

- Not framed as theft or loss, framed as strange, genuinely uncanny: a return without a returning subject 
    

- Appropriation/pastiche/détournement are knowing practices with articulated relations to source; fan cultures copy communally and citationally; GenAI's recurrence has neither of these features. It's a different kind of copying, and does that have its own potentials?  
    

- Rather than treating this only as a loss of authorial control, I want to ask what it opens up. Can this ownerless recurrence be redirected into appropriation in its fullest sense, as deliberate remix or détournement? Can GenAI become a tool for productive misreading rather than passive extraction? 
    

- In a system which is obscured from us – a castle of locked doors and empty crypts – can we make something in the gaps? 
    

- This difference in practice and potential practice is the interesting object, not a verdict or a moral position. 
    

CONCLUSION 

- Finish in a space of uncertainty: is this something to mourn, exploit, ignore, notice? 
    

- Can this be directed like detournment or does this import too much intentionality on an opaque process  
    

- Open questions:  
    

- Where's the line between an artist's generative process (however loosely I control it) and something closer to appropriation, when I can't see inside the model? 
    

- Does calling this "gothic" romanticise infrastructure that is extractive? 
    

- Can a practice of détournement exist without a stable object being détourned? 
    

- What would it even mean for a generative system to cite?
