Here are six gothic story premises:

**1. The Inherited House**  
A woman inherits a decaying estate from an aunt she never knew, only to discover the will requires her to live there for a full year before she can sell it. The portraits in the hallway seem to rearrange themselves each night, and the locals refuse to say why the previous owner never left the grounds alive.

**2. The Physician's Ward**  
A doctor at a remote Victorian asylum becomes obsessed with a patient who claims to remember her own death. As he digs into the asylum's records, he finds his own name in ledgers dating back a hundred years, in handwriting identical to his own.

**3. The Drowned Bride**  
On the eve of her wedding, a bride finds an old wedding dress in the attic, still damp, that fits her perfectly. As the wedding approaches, she begins losing time, waking near the lake with no memory, while guests whisper about the groom's first fiancée, who vanished the same way.

**4. The Cartographer's Debt**  
A mapmaker is hired to survey a fog-bound coastal village that appears in no official records and disappears from any map drawn of it within a week. He accepts a night's lodging from a gracious family, only to realize the village has been trying to map itself onto him.

**5. The Twin in the Wall**  
After her twin sister's death, a woman moves into their childhood home to settle the estate and starts hearing her sister's voice through the walls, giving her small, helpful warnings, each one true. She isn't sure whether she's being protected or slowly replaced.

**6. The Choir of St. Agnes**  
A restoration architect is hired to repair a fire-damaged church organ and finds that playing certain notes summons a choir no one else can hear, singing hymns older than the church itself, always one verse further along than they should be able to know.
