Latency, unidentified nodes and the web, physical cables and data centres.

Is the gap empty of have we learnt not how to perceive

Lecturer at Goldsmiths at Computational arts, and Camberwell Fine Art
Member of an arts and technologists collective called in-grid that works around the idea of systems and infrastructures
I'm an artist and am interested in authorship, unstable authorship, appropriation, piracy and collective authorship.
My thing am thinking about he genre of the gothic as a constructive cognitive model which has the potential to make legible issues of appropriation in generative ai systems. In particular, I argue that the gothic's vocabulary of unstable authorship and hidden architecture offers a productive lens for interrogating how generative AI reproduces style and trope without an author to claim them. Further to that where does it sit in relation to communal and personal practices in which pseudonymous/collective authorship is used as a starting position: when authorship is already treated as unstable by design?
